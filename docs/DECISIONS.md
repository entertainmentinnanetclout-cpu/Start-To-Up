# Decisions

1. **Innovation-only positioning:** structured publishing, onboarding commitment, reporting, and moderation reinforce scope.
2. **Private by default:** creators deliberately opt into wider visibility.
3. **No false IP guarantee:** timestamps, evidence, access controls, and auditability support creators but do not replace formal registration or legal advice.
4. **Foundation before live media:** the trustworthy social/project loop ships before livestreaming.
5. **Database-enforced authorization:** RLS and scoped helpers remain authoritative.
