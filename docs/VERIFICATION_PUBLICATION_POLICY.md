# Verification publication policy

Public Start To Up pages may display only the minimum trust information needed to confirm company standing, including the company registration number, enterprise status, confirmed tax-registration status, B-BBEE level and procurement recognition where supported by current source evidence.

Public pages must **not** publish:

- company registration/effective dates;
- certificate issue dates;
- certificate expiry dates;
- the SARS taxpayer reference number;
- director identity numbers;
- residential addresses;
- private verification source files or other non-essential identifiers from source certificates.

Registration, issue and expiry dates may be stored privately inside authenticated compliance/reminder workflows where operationally necessary, but they are not public-facing company trust data.

Start To Up uses its own neutral verification badges. CIPC, SARS, dtic/B-BBEE Commission or other regulator/public-body logos may be displayed only where the applicable published licence terms or written authorisation expressly permit the intended third-party commercial use.
