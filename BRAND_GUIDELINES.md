# Brand Guidelines

**Name:** Start To Up  
**Positioning:** The trusted network where innovation becomes visible, collaborative, and investable.

Use canonical assets in `public/brand`; never redraw, stretch, rotate, crowd, or arbitrarily recolor the symbol. The interface uses deep navy, electric blue, violet, and clean white. Favor generous whitespace, disciplined rounded surfaces, and progress/evidence cues over generic social decoration.

Voice is ambitious, credible, inclusive, and direct. Avoid unsupported claims that ideas are legally protected merely by posting them.
