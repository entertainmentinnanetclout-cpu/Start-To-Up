# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Trusted core social loop

- Session-aware navigation and protected routes
- Ten-step onboarding with versioned agreement acceptance
- Profiles, projects, posts, media, reactions, comments, saves, and follows
- Milestones, collaboration requests, and structured discovery
- Storage policies, abuse controls, and notifications

## Phase 2 — Collaboration and trust

- Teams, messaging, applications, and protected-access workflows
- Evidence-vault UI, attribution, reporting, moderation, and appeals
- Verified identities, organization profiles, investor discovery, and expert sessions

## Phase 3 — Media and ecosystem scale

- Project video, livestreams, webinars, and replay libraries
- Consent-aware program dashboards and recommendations
- Multilingual support, monetization, and mobile apps

Every phase includes accessibility, privacy, security, moderation, and measurable success criteria.
